# Product Analyst

**Fieldnote · Philadelphia, hybrid · Summer internship**

Fieldnote builds software for campus services teams. The product analyst intern helps the product manager see how a feature is used, write the next questions, and keep a small dashboard honest.

## What you will do

- Turn a messy operational spreadsheet into a short list of metrics the team reviews each week
- Write user stories from conversations with the people who run the service
- Sit with engineering to check that a requested report matches the workflow
- Point out where a form, a status, or a handoff is confusing
- Keep a record of decisions so the next sprint starts from the last answer

## What helps

- A class project where you owned a workflow, a schema, or a simple interface
- Excel or an intro dashboarding tool
- A habit of writing down what a stakeholder asked for, in their words and then in yours
