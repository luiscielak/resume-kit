# Business Systems Analyst

**Northline Insurance · Philadelphia, hybrid · Summer internship**

Northline is hiring a business systems analyst intern to sit with operations and turn how work actually happens into requirements a project team can build.

## What you will do

- Interview coordinators and branch staff about a current process
- Draw the process and write a short requirements brief
- Track open questions in a shared list the weekly meeting can use
- Pull a simple SQL query when the team needs a count, a status, or an exception list
- Explain the process back to both operations and the technical team in plain language

## What helps

- Coursework or a project in systems analysis, databases, or business process
- Comfort with Excel and basic SQL
- Evidence that you have listened to the people who do the work and written it down
